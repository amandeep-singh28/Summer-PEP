(ii)Plan & Execute Pattern
In the Plan & Execute Pattern, the AI agent performs two distinct phases:
- Planning Phase
    Understand the user's goal.
    Break the task into smaller steps.
    Decide the order in which the steps should be executed.
- Execution Phase
    Execute each step one by one.
    Use tools whenever required.
    Produce the final output.

Question
    |
Plan
    |
Step 1
    |
Step 2
    |
Output

(iii)TOT(Tree of thoughts)
The Tree of Thoughts pattern enables an AI agent to:
- Generate multiple possible ideas (thoughts).
- Evaluate each idea.
- Keep the best ones.
- Discard poor ones.
- Continue exploring until it reaches the best solution.

             Problem Statement
                    │
                    ▼
            Generate Multiple Ideas
            ┌──────────┬──────────┬
            ▼          ▼          ▼

            ▼          ▼          ▼

            │          │          │

                Continue Reasoning
